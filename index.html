Mission Briefing

How to Complete Your Expedition

Educational Mission: Demonstrate why coordinating all four elements of the Marketing Mix (Product, Price, Place, and Promotion) is essential for business success and customer value.

  • Three-Room Structure: You can enter Room 1 (The Supply Depot) and Room 2 (The Field Observatory) in any order from the Navigation Map. Room 3 (The Command Tent) starts locked.
  • Collecting Document Fragments: Solving all questions in Room 1 reveals Fragment 1. Solving all questions in Room 2 reveals Fragment 2.
  • Unlocking Room 3: Once both torn fragments are collected, they join together on the Navigation Map to reveal a four-digit access code. Enter that code to unlock Room 3 and complete the final challenge.
  • Timer & Life Points: You have 20 minutes and start with 100 Life Points. Each incorrect answer deducts 10 Life Points. Optional hints are available on every challenge without penalty.
